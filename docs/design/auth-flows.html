Account flows — design preview

Template layout and Poppins type, in 9 Solar Home green. Nothing here is live yet — this is for sign-off before building. Scroll each row sideways on a phone.

A · Creating an account (self sign-up)

Someone signs up on their own. People invited by a project manager use the same first two screens, then go straight into the app with the role the PM gave them — they never see A3 or A4.

B · Forgot password

From the sign-in screen. Codes are 6 digits (that is what our sign-in provider, Clerk, sends) and expire after 10 minutes.

C · Approving a request (project manager)

The PM gets an email + in-app alert, reviews the request, grants a role (they can change what was asked for) or declines with a note. The applicant then gets an email and a WhatsApp message.

Things to confirm

  • Header colour in Black mode is a deeper green (#0E7F53) so white text stays readable; Light mode uses the app's #0A9A63.
  • Mobile is asked at sign-up (with the country picker) because approvals and updates go out by WhatsApp.
